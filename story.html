The Researcher

Teaching machines to write software

I'm an AI researcher at FPT Software AI Center, working under the guidance of Prof. Bach Le and Dr. Nghi D. Q. Bui. I graduated early with an excellent Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

My research lives at the intersection of AI and Software Engineering — training and fine-tuning Large Language Models for code, and building agentic systems that can reason about, document, and even migrate entire codebases.
