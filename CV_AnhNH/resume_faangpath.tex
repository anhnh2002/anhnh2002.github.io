\begin{rSection}{Research Interests}

My current research focuses on AI for Software Engineering, particularly foundation
models for code, autonomous coding agents, and long-horizon software evolution. I aim
to study principled approaches for training and adapting code-focused language models,
as well as designing reliable agentic systems for repository-scale software engineering
tasks. I am also open to diversifying my research across related areas in the future.

\end{rSection}
